Step-by-Step Reporting Guide

Before you pick up the phone, walk through these three questions to find your correct reporting path:

  1. Check for an Immediate Danger

    Seconds count

    Is someone injured? Is there an active fire? Are you witnessing an active assault or someone intentionally setting fire to a structure/brush with fireworks?

    • Yes: Stop here. Call 911 immediately.
    • No: Proceed to Step 2.

  2. Identify Your Location Jurisdiction

    Rules vary by town

    Are you located on Bainbridge Island, or somewhere else in Kitsap County?

    • Bainbridge Island: Proceed to Step 3.
    • All other areas (Bremerton, Poulsbo, Port Orchard, unincorporated Kitsap): Law enforcement will not respond in person to non-emergencies. Go directly online to submit a report for statistical tracking at kitsap911.org/fireworks (Portal open June 27, 9:00 AM through July 6, 11:59 PM).

  3. Bainbridge Island Direct Hotline

    Special holiday hours

    If you are on Bainbridge Island and have a specific address or location for the illegal fireworks:

    • On July 3rd, 4th, and 5th (5:00 PM to 1:00 AM), call their non-emergency hotline directly at 206-842-5211.
    • Outside of those specific dates and times, use the county-wide online tool at kitsap911.org/fireworks.
